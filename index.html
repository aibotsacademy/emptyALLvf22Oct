Empty-leg monetization

Turn repositioning into revenue.

Every one-way trip leaves an aircraft somewhere it doesn’t need to be. AERO finds those legs, checks them against the schedule, matches demand and recommends a price you can explain.

  1. 01
    Detect the routeRepositioning legs are found automatically from the schedule — explicit ferries and implied returns to base.
  2. 02
    Match demandRoute searches, open CRM leads and saved passenger routes are scored against each leg.
  3. 03
    Quote with reasonsA recommended price with its assumptions — never below direct operating cost.
  4. 04
    Recover revenuePublished legs feed the marketplace and broker inquiries flow into the CRM.
