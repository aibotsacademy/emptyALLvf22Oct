    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    How is the monthly fee calculated?

    A fixed platform base for your plan plus a rate for each aircraft under management. Helicopters and jets are billed the same. Users are unlimited on every plan.

    How is the implementation paid?

    50% when the agreement is signed and 50% at go-live. Monthly billing starts at go-live, not at signature.

    What happens if I add or remove aircraft?

    Aircraft can be added at any time and are prorated in the next invoice. Reductions apply at the next renewal. Adding aircraft beyond your plan's range moves you to the next plan.

    Is there a discount for annual billing?

    Yes — paying the monthly fee annually in advance gives you two months free (about 17% off). The implementation fee is not affected.

    Can I move from Essentials to Operator later?

    Yes. Upgrades only charge the difference in implementation, and any add-on modules you already pay for are absorbed into the new plan.

    Are integrations included?

    Enterprise includes up to three authorized integrations. On other plans each integration (scheduling, flight tracking, maintenance tracking, payments or CRM) is quoted as a one-time build plus a small monthly maintenance fee.
