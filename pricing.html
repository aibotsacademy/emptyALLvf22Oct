Your systems, connected.

The AERO Open API exposes inventory, quotes, bookings and trip status over REST, with webhooks for every change. Brokers get it by default; operators can add it or have us build the connectors for them.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    How is the monthly fee calculated?

    A fixed platform base for your plan plus a rate for each aircraft. Enterprise uses volume bands, so aircraft 41–70 and 71–100 cost less each. Helicopters and jets are billed the same, and users are unlimited.

    How does the broker fee work?

    On the hybrid model you pay 35% less per aircraft and a 4% fee on bookings confirmed through the AERO Marketplace, AeroFlow or the API. The Broker plan has no per-aircraft fee: a fixed API access fee plus 5% of each confirmed booking. No confirmed booking, no fee. Fees are calculated on the charter price before taxes.

    What can I integrate through the API?

    Inventory search, quotes, booking requests, trip status and webhooks are available through the AERO Open API. We also build connectors to your scheduling, flight tracking, maintenance, payments, CRM or accounting systems, when those systems offer an authorized API.

    We manage more than 100 aircraft.

    The public estimator goes up to 100 aircraft. Above that we prepare a custom agreement with its own volume pricing — ask the proposal agent and mention your fleet size.

    What is GEO and do you guarantee AI citations?

    Generative Engine Optimization structures your content, schema markup and third-party mentions so AI assistants can understand and cite you. We report on visibility monthly, but no one can guarantee placement in Google or in any AI assistant.

    How is the implementation paid?

    50% when the agreement is signed and 50% at go-live. Monthly billing starts at go-live, not at signature. Annual prepayment of the monthly fee gives you two months free.

    What happens with the data I give the proposal agent?

    It is used only to prepare and send your proposal, and is not shared with third parties. The agent does not make commitments: the final price is always confirmed in a written proposal.
